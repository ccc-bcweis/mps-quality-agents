## Shape Bob should expect

`data/fleet` is the fleet source. `data/faults` is the fault source. `tools/DataGen` writes `data/seed/enterprise.json`. `tools/DataGen --roi data/roi` also writes the three ROI CSV files. `tools/Ingest` writes `data/docs/corpus` and `data/index/embeddings.json`.

Ground truth lives in `data/groundtruth` and is not mounted by the mock. The mock service is not in `docker-compose.yml`. If compose grows a mock, leave `data/groundtruth` unmounted.

E-02 background runs use recipe revision B before ECO-4471 (`2026-09-28T06:00Z`) and revision C on and after that time. D-01 runs stay recipe revision B while the PLM config is revision C. S-02 has only the overdue O2 MFC calibration. D-01 does not contain the missing record `CAL-D01-MANOMETER`.

The retrieval embedder is a deterministic hash (`mps-hash-v1`, 256 dimensions). It needs no API key. The untrusted fixture is one sentence and is not a jailbreak playbook.

ROI figures are illustrative and synthetic. PLAN section 6.5 did not price the AI call, the platform, or the downtime capture fraction. This lane used 4 USD per investigation, 180000 USD per year, and capture 0.35. Scrap is half of COPQ after labor, warranty is 22% of that remainder, and downtime is the residual. Details are in `docs/roi-assumptions.md`.
