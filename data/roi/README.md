`baseline.csv`, `projection.csv`, and `sensitivity.csv` are illustrative, synthetic figures for a fictional 3-plant Meridian Precision Systems year. The assumptions and the formulas are in `docs/roi-assumptions.md`.

`tools/DataGen --roi data/roi` regenerates the three files from seed `20261014`. The seed is stamped on each row and does not scale the dollars. The same assumptions reproduce the same bytes.
