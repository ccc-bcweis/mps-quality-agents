# MPS-D7 preventive maintenance

Copyright (c) 2026 Bryan C. Weis and Common Consulting Company

This SOP covers fictional MPS-D7 deposition tools D-01 and D-02.

## Deposit pressure

During PECVD oxide deposit, hold chamber pressure inside 3-4.5 Torr. Record the manometer reading before releasing the tool. A missing manometer calibration record is not replaced by a heater-thermocouple certificate.

## Showerhead

Confirm the PLM showerhead revision matches the part installed on the tool. Do not overwrite a MES recipe header to force agreement. If the revisions disagree, escalate and keep both records.
