# O2 mass flow controller calibration procedure

Copyright (c) 2026 Bryan C. Weis and Common Consulting Company

## As found

1. Record the controller serial, the tool, and the chamber.
2. Read as-found oxygen flow against the standard ash window 1500-2500 sccm.
3. If the due date has passed and no completion time is stored, the record stays overdue.

## Closeout

A completed calibration writes the completion time and clears the overdue flag. A certificate for a different gas mixture does not close this procedure. File the work under the calibration id for that controller.
