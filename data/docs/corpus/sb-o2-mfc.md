# Service bulletin for an overdue O2 mass flow controller

Copyright (c) 2026 Bryan C. Weis and Common Consulting Company

An overdue O2 mass flow controller on MPS-S7 leaves oxygen flow outside the ash window. The standard ash step window is 1500-2500 sccm. Tool S-02 chamber A is the scope when only that chamber steps.

A supplier notice for a different gas can arrive the day before and still sit inside its own spec. That notice does not close the overdue O2 mass flow controller record. Complete the calibration or keep the controller blocked.

Do not copy this bulletin onto an etch tool. Etch gas lots are a separate material path.
