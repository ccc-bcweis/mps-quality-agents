# MPS-S7 preventive maintenance

Copyright (c) 2026 Bryan C. Weis and Common Consulting Company

This SOP covers fictional MPS-S7 strip tools S-01 and S-02.

## Oxygen flow

On the standard ash step, hold oxygen flow inside 1500-2500 sccm. Check the mass flow controller due date before the cassette starts. An overdue controller stays open until the calibration is completed.

## Preventive maintenance

Inspect the remote plasma source and write the work order id on the lot traveler. A supplier notice for a different gas is not a substitute for the overdue controller record.
